=== STYLE BIBLE: STEAMS OF THE MACHINE ===

Pixel art, 16-bit SNES JRPG style, reminiscent of The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past and Chrono Trigger. Theme: Victorian Industrial Steampunk — heavy brass, burnished copper, weathered leather, and coal-powered machinery. The overall aesthetic is gritty yet polished, combining ornate Victorian craftsmanship with rugged industrial machinery.

Limited 24-color palette strictly enforced: Polished Brass (#C8860A), Burnished Copper (#A06820), Antique Bronze (#7A4A18), Oxidized Copper (#6B3A10), Dark Mahogany (#4A2A08), Weathered Leather (#8C6040), Iron Gray (#5A5A5A), Coal Black (#3A3A3A), Deep Black (#1E1E1E), Smoke (#2C2C2C), Dark Wood (#4A3020), Rust (#6A5040), Ochre (#C87820), Furnace Orange (#E8A030), Warm Amber (#F0C060), Forest Green (#4A7A40), Dark Green (#3A6030), Verdigris (#5A8A50), Steam White (#D0D0C8), Light Gray Steam (#A0A098), Oil Spill Purple (#2A1A3A), Deep Oil Black (#1A1428), Damage Crimson (#E05020), Background White (#FFFFFF).

Technical specs: 1-pixel dark outline on all sprites and tiles. Soft checkered dithering for shadows and light transitions. No anti-aliasing — pure pixel art. Light source fixed at upper-left; shadows fall toward lower-right. Centered subject on uniform white background (#FFFFFF). No text, no UI, no borders, no watermarks.

The visual vocabulary of this world includes visible spinning gears, copper valves with pressure gauges, thick pipes with brass fittings, rivets and bolts on all metal structures, smokestacks emitting dense white steam, brass goggles, weathered leather duster coats, wide-brimmed hats, open-fingered leather gloves, mechanical exoskeletons, plague doctor masks with segmented brass plating, orange furnace glows behind lenses and openings, grease stains, oil spills in deep purple and black tones, and subtle scuff marks on all surfaces.
