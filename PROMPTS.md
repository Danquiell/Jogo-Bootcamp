## FASE NOVA

=== STYLE BIBLE: [STEAMPUNK FLOOR TILESET 16-BIT] ===

Visual Style:

    Era: 16-bit SNES JRPG (Zelda: ALttP / Chrono Trigger).

    Perspective: Pure top-down (90°) or slight 3/4 overhead.

    Resolution Density: Designed for a 320x180 native resolution. Assets must maintain a consistent "pixel-per-unit" ratio where a standard character would occupy roughly 16x24 or 24x24 pixels.

Technical Specs for Godot Integration:

    Tiling: Must be seamlessly tileable on all axes.

    Grid Alignment: Patterns should suggest a 16x16 or 32x32 pixel grid to match standard JRPG tilemaps.

    Scale: High-frequency detail (bolts, small gears) must be legible at 1x zoom.

Color Palette (24-Color Industrial):

    Metals: Burnished Copper, Polished Brass, Rusted Iron (3 shades each).

    Aesthetics: Dark Mahogany, Verdigris (oxidation), Oil Spills (Deep Purples/Blacks).

    Lights: Warm Amber (low intensity) for floor-recessed lights.

Rendering & Texture:

    Outline: 1-pixel dark sepia/burnt umber internal outlines to define plates and grates.

    Dithering: Heavy use of checkered dithering for shadows in the recesses of floor grates and metallic gradients.

    Surface: Mix of smooth polished metal, rough anti-slip industrial plating (diamond plate), and semi-transparent iron floor grates.

Composition:

    A flat, square section of floor texture.

    No background elements: Just the floor asset itself.

    No perspective distortion: The "camera" is perfectly flat to the ground.

Constraints:

    NO walls, NO UI, NO text, NO watermarks.

    NO high-contrast highlights that would distract from the Player sprite.

=== END STYLE ===

A seamless 16-bit pixel art floor texture for a steampunk engine room. Top-down perspective, designed for a 320x180 resolution game. The texture features a modular layout of riveted copper plates, dark iron ventilation grates with dithered shadows showing depth underneath, and small brass pipes recessed into the floor. Occasional grease stains and subtle scuff marks. 1-pixel dark outlines, 24-color warm metallic palette. The image must be perfectly tileable on all sides. Flat lighting with no walls or furniture. Set against a uniform white background for easy cropping.
